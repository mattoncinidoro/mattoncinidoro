Privacy policy

Information under Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR"). Last updated: 27 September 2026. If this English version and the Italian version differ, the Italian version prevails.

1. Data controller

Lorenzo Viani, La Spezia, Italy. Email: mattoncinidoro@gmail.com.
"Mattoncini d'Oro" is a hobby project made up of this website and the Telegram channel t.me/mattoncinidoro.

2. In short

  • This website uses no cookies, has no contact forms and uses no analytics or advertising tools.
  • We do not ask for or keep personal data of channel subscribers.
  • When you click an affiliate link you go to a shop's website, which handles your data under its own privacy policy.

3. Data processed and purposes

a) Visiting the website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). To serve pages and keep them secure, GitHub automatically logs the IP address and technical request data (browser, page, date and time). Legal basis: legitimate interest in running and securing the site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We have no access to these logs, which GitHub keeps for a limited period under its privacy statement.

b) Telegram channel

You join the channel through Telegram, which processes your account data as an independent controller under its own privacy policy. As channel administrators, Telegram shows us the number of subscribers, aggregate statistics and, within the limits of the app, the list of subscribers with their public name. We do not export, store or use this data for any other purpose, and we do not send private messages to subscribers. The channel bot only publishes posts and does not collect data about readers.

c) Contacting us by email or Telegram

If you write to us, we use your address or username and the content of your message only to reply. Legal basis: your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in replying. We keep messages as long as needed to handle the request and no longer than 24 months.

d) Affiliate links

Some links lead to shops through affiliate programs (Amazon, Awin, Rakuten Advertising). When you click them, the shop or affiliate network may set cookies or similar tools on its own site, subject to the consent it asks you for, to credit a purchase to us. These parties act as independent controllers. We only receive aggregate reports from them (clicks, sales and commissions), with no data that identifies you.

  • Amazon: Amazon.it privacy notice
  • Awin: Awin privacy policy
  • Rakuten Advertising: Rakuten Advertising privacy policy

4. Recipients and transfers outside the EU

The data described above may be processed by GitHub, Inc. and Telegram, including outside the European Economic Area. For GitHub, transfers to the United States rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. We do not sell or share personal data with third parties.

5. Your rights

You can ask at any time for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interest (Articles 15-22 GDPR) by writing to mattoncinidoro@gmail.com. You can leave the Telegram channel at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or the authority of your EU country.

6. Children

The channel and website are aimed at adults. Telegram sets a minimum age for using its service.

7. Changes

We will update this page if our processing changes, for example when we join new affiliate programs. The date at the top shows the latest change.
