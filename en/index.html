How it works

From price to post in three steps

We find the deals

The bot checks LEGO® set prices every day at Italian and European online shops.

We score them

An algorithm weighs several factors and gives the set a 0-100 score for its collector potential.

We post the best

Only deals scoring 60 or more reach the channel, with photo, price and link.

The score

One algorithm, several factors

The score combines several factors, weighted by how much they affect a set's value over time. Data comes from public sources and from the price history collected by the bot.

⏳

Retirement

How soon the set is expected to leave the market.

🏷️

Discount

The deal price versus the European list price.

🧑‍🚀

Exclusive minifigures

The value of minifigures found only in that set.

📉

Price history

Whether today's price is truly low compared with previous months.

🔥 Don't miss it 85+
🟢 Great deal 70-84
🟡 Good opportunity 60-69

Retirement dates are estimates and minifigure values change over time. The score is guidance for collectors, not financial advice and not a guarantee that a set will gain value.

Disclosure

Affiliate links, plainly

Many links in the channel are affiliate links: if you buy through one, the shop pays us a small commission. The price you pay does not change, and commissions cover the costs of the server and data.

Shops do not pay to be featured and have no influence on the score, which is calculated the same way for every deal.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

We also take part in affiliate programs run by networks such as Awin and Rakuten Advertising. Prices are those found at the time of posting and may change: the price shown by the shop is the one that applies.
