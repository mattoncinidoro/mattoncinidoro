LEGO® deals picked with a collector's score

Mattoncini d'Oro ("Golden Bricks") is a free Telegram channel, in Italian, that posts discounts on LEGO® sets and gives each deal a 0-100 score for its collector potential. Only deals scoring 60 or more are published.

Join the Telegram channel

What the channel does

A bot checks LEGO® set prices at Italian and European online shops, compares them with the list price and with price history, gathers retirement and minifigure data, and computes a score. Each post shows the set photo, price, shop, score with its rating band, and a link to buy.

The channel is aimed at adult collectors who want to buy at the right moment: when a set is about to retire, is heavily discounted, or includes minifigures found only in that set.

How the score works (0-100)

The score is computed by an algorithm that combines several factors, weighted by how much they affect a set's collector value. Data comes from public sources and from the price history collected by the bot.

⏳ Retirement

How soon the set is expected to leave the market.

🏷️ Discount

The deal price versus the European list price.

🧑‍🚀 Exclusive minifigures

The value of minifigures found only in that set.

📉 Price history

Whether today's price is truly low compared with previous months.

Rating bands

🔥 Don't miss it85-100
🟢 Great deal70-84
🟡 Good opportunity60-69
Not publishedbelow 60

Retirement dates are estimates and minifigure values change over time. The score is guidance for collectors, not financial advice and not a guarantee that a set will gain value.

Affiliate links and disclosure

Many links posted in the channel are affiliate links: if you buy through one, the shop pays us a small commission. The price you pay does not change. Commissions cover the costs of the server and data.

Shops do not pay to be featured and have no influence on the score, which is calculated the same way for every deal.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

We also take part in affiliate programs run by networks such as Awin and Rakuten Advertising. Prices are those found at the time of posting and may change: the price shown by the shop is the one that applies.
